Privacy

We collect as little as we can, and say plainly what we keep.

Your account

When you sign up we store your email, the name you choose, and a hashed password (never the password itself) on our server. A login cookie keeps you signed in for 30 days.

Cats you send

The cat’s name, address, dates, coat, story, and the first photo (shrunk to 512 px) are stored on our server so anyone with the link can visit its home.

Lanterns and messages

Lanterns and messages are stored on our server, and everyone visiting the cat’s home can see them. Messages show the name on your account. To count lanterns once per person, we keep a random ID in a cookie for visitors who are not logged in.

Translation

So that both Chinese and English readers can read them, stories and messages are translated automatically when you submit them. The text is sent to an AI model through Vercel AI Gateway for this, and the translation is saved next to the original.

Contact

To remove a cat or ask about your data, reach us through the About page (an email address is coming soon).

隐私说明

我们尽量少收集，也尽量说清楚。

你的账号

注册时，我们会在服务器上保存你的邮箱、你填写的名字，以及加密处理后的密码（不保存密码原文）。登录后会用一个 Cookie 让你保持登录 30 天。

你送来的猫

猫的名字、主页地址、日期、花色、故事和第一张照片（缩小到 512 像素）会保存在服务器上，这样拿到链接的人都能去它家看看。

点灯和留言

点灯和留言保存在服务器上，来看这只猫的人都能看到。留言会显示你账号上的名字。为了让每人只点一次灯，没登录的访客会有一个随机编号保存在 Cookie 里。

翻译

为了让中文和英文读者都能看懂，故事和留言提交时会自动翻译：文字会通过 Vercel AI Gateway 发给 AI 模型翻译，译文和原文一起保存。

联系

想删除某只猫、或对数据有疑问，请通过「关于」页联系我们（邮箱稍后补上）。
