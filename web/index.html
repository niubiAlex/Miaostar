How to send a cat

Free, and no drawing skills needed. Leave photos and a story — they will fly over by spaceship.

1. Upload a few photosA clear face shot and a full-body side shot work best — we draw from them.
2. Write their storyName, birthday, the day they came home, and what you want to say.
3. They land on Cat PlanetAfter a little while in flight, they move into their own cottage in town. Visit anytime; friends can light a lantern.
