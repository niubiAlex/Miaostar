About Cat Planet

Every cat that left us has a little home here.

Cat Planet is a quiet place. Cats have their own cottages, fish by the pier, stretch on the grass. You can visit, light a lantern, and leave a note.

What works today

  • Walk the town and plaza
  • Visit Uncle at No. 1 — everyone’s cat; anyone can pet, call and feed him
  • Create an account, send a cat, and claim a unique address like /momo
  • Light a lantern and leave a message in a cat’s home — everyone can see them
  • Hear them: pick one of five real cat voices for meows and purrs

关于喵星

每一只离开的猫，都在这里有一间小屋。

喵星是一个安静的地方。猫在这里有自己的小屋，在栈桥上钓鱼，在草地上伸懒腰。你可以回来看看它，为它点一盏灯，留一句话。

现在能做什么

  • 逛喵城镇和广场
  • 去 1 号看看叔叔，它是大家的猫，谁都可以摸它、叫它、喂它
  • 注册账号，送你的猫来喵星，领一个专属地址，比如 /momo
  • 在猫的小屋里点灯、留言，大家都能看到
  • 听它的声音：五种真猫录音，喵声和呼噜都有
